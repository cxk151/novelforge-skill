# Continuity audit

Audit five levels:

- Fact: names, aliases, age, occupation, relationships, status, possession, location, injuries, dates, travel, layout, and world rules.
- Knowledge: how and when each character learned a fact; distinguish character and reader knowledge.
- Causality: motive, capability, opportunity, mechanism, consequence, and evidence provenance.
- Narrative: repeated entries, stalled pressure, premature resolution, missing settlement, abandoned relationships, unearned reversals, and unchanged scenes.
- Open threads: planned, planted, reinforced, due, resolved, or abandoned, with origin and expected resolution window.

Classify findings as critical, serious, moderate, or advisory. Cite chapter/file evidence. Distinguish definite contradiction from ambiguity. Recommend the smallest repair and downstream effects.
