# Serialization and handoff

Track published/draft status, total words, schedule, backlog, current volume/event progress, and next deliverables. Treat dates and targets as plans unless confirmed.

When reader feedback or analytics are supplied, group signals into logic, character, pacing, payoff, expectation, style, and noise. Look for repeated signals and chapter-level changes; do not overturn canon because of one comment.

For real-event adaptation, identify the social conflict rather than copying the incident. Separate verified facts, allegations, and invention. Change identifying names, institutions, places, and distinctive details; design an original intervention, evidence chain, escalation, and resolution.

A handoff must include active constraints, authority order, current status, confirmed manuscript range, recent summaries, character states, current event, timeline anchor, open threads, recent changes, superseded material, next task, protected content, and known risks.

Support manuscript, volume, bible, outline, audit, comparison, and handoff exports. For formatted documents, use the relevant document skill and visually verify layout.
