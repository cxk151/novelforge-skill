# Hierarchical planning

Use the smallest level that answers the request: premise/theme, master outline, volume, arc/phase, event, chapter, then scene card.

An event outline should cover entry, protagonist reason to engage, competing interests, opponent leverage, protagonist disadvantage, evidence/resources, escalation, reversal mechanism, consequence, aftermath, and next-story interfaces.

Before accepting a plan, test causality, agency, escalation, repetition, information flow, opponent competence, consequence, and length budget. Present route alternatives with advantages and risks only when a choice is genuinely open.

A chapter plan must state inheritance from the previous chapter, objective, active agent, state change, reader reward, ending condition, required beats, forbidden outcomes, target words, and continuity dependencies.

Do not manufacture a cliffhanger every chapter. Alternate revelation, decision, reversal, threat, emotional residue, relationship shift, payoff interruption, and new complication.
