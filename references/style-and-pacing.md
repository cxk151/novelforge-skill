# Style and pacing

Build a project voice profile from confirmed prose: narrative distance, sentence/paragraph rhythm, dialogue ratio, description density, interiority, humor mechanism, profanity boundary, system-message frequency, and ending behavior.

Flag patterns rather than isolated phrases: repeated atmospheric openings, over-explained emotions, symmetrical paragraphs, mechanical turn-taking, generic reaction crowds, recap after demonstration, uniform diction, adjective stacking, and constant aphorisms. Do not replace them with new canned phrases.

Dialogue must perform an action. Let pauses, mistakes, interruptions, object handling, and selective attention carry subtext.

Assess conflict, information, emotion, character movement, suspense, and payoff across a range. A payoff normally needs setup, pressure, countermove, escalation, release, concrete settlement, and aftermath. Vary payoff and entry types.

Never imitate a living author's distinctive style; convert requests into transferable traits.
