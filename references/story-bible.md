# Story bible

Maintain structured, changeable state rather than decorative biographies.

## Characters

Track stable ID, canonical name, aliases, age/time anchor, role, first appearance, location/status, goals, pressures, resources, secrets, relationships, speech traits, capabilities, injuries, and knowledge boundaries. Give recurring characters distinct incentives and response patterns.

## World rules

Track geography, travel time, institutions, law/custom, economy, technology, history, social assumptions, and exceptions. For speculative settings also track power tiers, costs, ceilings, scarcity, advancement, countermeasures, and public knowledge.

## Systems and powers

For each ability record source, acquisition, first use, conditions, cost, cooldown, level, observers, upgrades, and prohibitions. New functionality needs setup or an explicit reveal; do not retrofit an unlimited solution.

## Items and factions

Track item owner, location, condition, charges, restrictions, and last use. Track faction goals, resources, internal divisions, public face, secrets, relationships, and leverage.

Update state after an accepted chapter. Historical facts remain immutable; current-state fields may change with chapter provenance.
